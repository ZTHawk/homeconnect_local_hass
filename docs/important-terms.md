These are important terms that may be used in this repo and here are their definitions, more terms may be added over time.

- Hc = Short for Home Connect.
- Home Connect Cloud = The core [Home Connect integration](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/home_connect/)
- Ws = Websocket
- [BSH](https://www.bsh-group.com/us/) = It is the parent company of of the brands that use Home Connect
