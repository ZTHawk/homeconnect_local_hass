## Home Connect Local

[![Latest release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/vemboy200/homeconnect_local_hass?label=latest%20release)](https://github.com/vemboy200/homeconnect_local_hass/releases/latest)
[![Latest beta](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/vemboy200/homeconnect_local_hass?include_prereleases&label=latest%20beta)](https://github.com/vemboy200/homeconnect_local_hass/releases)
[![Quality Scale](https://img.shields.io/badge/quality%20scale-🏆Platinum-brightgreen)](https://www.home-assistant.io/docs/quality_scale/#-platinum)

Control your Bosch/Siemens [**Home Connect**](https://www.home-connect.com/global) appliances (dishwashers, washers/dryers, ovens, hobs/hoods, coffee makers, fridges/freezers) directly over your local network, so no cloud round-trip required for day-to-day use.

> [!NOTE]
> This isn't something Home Connect has authorized for third-party use, and the endpoints involved are undocumented and could change without notice. If that's not something you're comfortable with, use the core [Home Connect](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/home_connect/) integration instead.

**Requirements:**
- A Home Connect account with your appliance(s) added
- Your appliance and Home Assistant on the same local network

**Setup:** sign in with your Home Connect account, or upload a profile file from the [Home Connect Profile Downloader](https://github.com/bruestel/homeconnect-profile-downloader). These instructions are covered in the docs.

For installation details, supported devices, the full entity list, troubleshooting, and automation examples, see the [documentation](https://github.com/vemboy200/homeconnect_local_hass/blob/beta/docs/README.md).
